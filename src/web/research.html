When and why do electricity prices turn negative in Spain?

Negative prices are a recent feature of the available Spanish day-ahead history. In this snapshot, they first appear in 2024 and are concentrated in daylight delivery periods. The data shows when the outcome occurs; it does not, by itself, establish that renewable output or demand caused it.

Method and limits

The snapshot uses Spanish OMIE day-ahead observations from 1 January 2018 through 20 August 2026. A negative observation is one with a price below 0 €/MWh. Hourly and 15-minute native observations are counted as published; no interpolation or gap filling is applied. Solar, wind, demand, imports and exports are not currently joined to this research note, so causal explanations remain hypotheses for a later data phase.
